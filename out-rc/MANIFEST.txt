WHAT IT IS

The first candidate that needs no shell command to produce data. diag3 proved the firmware can
read the real L7 and put its grid frames on CAN, and it needed `tofdiag arm` to do it. This
removes that dependency and adds the three things that stood between it and something a fleet
could boot: a boot-time L7 recovery, a watchdog that notices when work stops, and a record saying
why when it does.

WHAT IT IS NOT. Not a release. Still DEV, still safety-lidar bypassed, still unconfirmed, and the
robot is still not driveable. It is also not a diagnostic image: `tofdiag status`, `tofdiag i2c`
and the I2C forensics block exist only under TOF_DIAG_HANG, which this does not define. The
`orphan_rxne` counter cannot be read here.

CHECKS ON THE ARTEFACT

  262,144 B; image_ok byte at size-24 = 0xff; MCUboot magic tail 77c295f360d2ef7f3552500f2cb67980
  The .test.bin is the confirmed image with EXACTLY ONE byte changed -- verified by differencing
  the two rather than by trusting the recipe: `cmp -l` reports one line, at offset 262120
  (0-based), 0x01 -> 0xff.

  boot_write_img_confirmed: 0 symbols in the ELF. The self-confirm path is not merely skipped at
  runtime, it is absent from the image, so any reset of any kind hands the board back to the
  previous one. That is the single build flag -- TOF_DEV_NO_AUTO_CONFIRM -- separating this from
  the eventual deliverable; nothing else differs.

  NO-FAT enforced twice: CMake refuses any ENABLE_TOF_L7_ULD build whose Kconfig came back with
  CONFIG_DISK_ACCESS, DISK_DRIVER_SDMMC, FILE_SYSTEM, FAT_FILESYSTEM_ELM, FILE_SYSTEM_SHELL or
  FS_FATFS_LFN enabled, and the Makefile re-checks the .config that was produced.

  Exactly one wdt_feed() call site, in tof_watchdog_feeder.cpp, enforced at configure time for
  every image. In the binary, state_controller::static_poll_1s_callback is a single `bx lr`:
  nothing is left in the interrupt path at all.

  DTCM is 124 KB and FORENSICS is a separate 4 KB region; the tombstone at 0x2001FF00 asserts its
  own bounds against FORENSICS rather than against DTCM.

TESTS

  13 host suites green at this commit, including the four this work added: tof_progress 6/6,
  tof_task_watchdog 22/22, tof_watchdog_tombstone 16/16, tof_watchdog_feeder 1/1 (one ordered
  scenario), plus the three recovery suites at 28/28 and tof_acquisition 81/81.

  Every load-bearing rule is mutation-checked. Named here because a passing suite says less than a
  suite that fails when the rule is removed: dropping the alive guard, letting a probe error fall
  through to a stop, moving the recovery after the pins, restoring the L7 baseline requirement,
  sharing one timestamp between the two send slots, dropping the silence half, suspending
  everything during a long operation, removing the cap from the waiting phase, inverting the send
  slot discrimination, undoing the feed latch, admitting a never-ACKed sender to the baseline,
  removing the phase assignment on a refused feed, never resetting the consecutive-failure count,
  and putting the timer feed back.

WHAT HAS TO HAPPEN BEFORE ANYTHING IS CONCLUDED FROM THIS IMAGE

  The tombstone region must be shown readable and writable at 0x2001FF00 on real hardware. Cross
  compiling proves the address is outside what the linker allocates from; it proves nothing about
  the MPU, and if the MPU refuses, the record silently does not exist and every later conclusion
  drawn from its absence is wrong.

  The feeder's 1 KiB stack is chosen, not measured.

  Whether stopping a ranging session clears what actually survives an SCB reset is unknown. The
  recovery acts on the one surviving-state candidate a running SCB can act on. A pass reporting
  both positions `absent` on a warm reset is evidence against the reasoning the ordering rests on,
  not a quiet success.

  See ../../allmemory/hanging_object/rc_recovery/onboard_checklist_draft.md for the procedure, and
  decode_tombstone.py beside it for reading the record back through the E3 shell's devmem.
